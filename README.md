# Election Stage

Create an extremely polished, premium, Apple-inspired interactive campaign website for a school pre-election campaign.

The website will be demonstrated to students on an iPad. It should feel like an Apple product launch mixed with a sophisticated interactive campaign experience.

The goal is to make the experience extremely visually impressive, fast, funny, interactive, and memorable.

CORE STRUCTURE

There are TWO completely separate election interfaces:

1. Boy Vice President

2. Girl Vice President

The student completes the Boy Vice President section first, then proceeds to the Girl Vice President section.

The two sections should feel like separate stages of the same premium experience.

Do NOT create a complicated voting backend.

This is primarily an interactive campaign presentation.

After each student finishes, I will simply reload the website to start again. Therefore, there is NO need for a reset button.

BOY VICE PRESIDENT

Candidates:

Muhammad Fasih Ur Rehman

Symbol: Crocodile

This is the campaign's preferred candidate.

The crocodile should be represented by a clean, recognizable crocodile graphic. Do NOT directly copy or reproduce the Lacoste logo. Use an original crocodile illustration/icon with a premium appearance.

Abu Bakr

Symbol: Football

Muhammad Qamar

Symbol: Globe

Display all three candidates prominently.

However, this is a CAMPAIGN website, not a neutral government voting portal.

The design should allow campaign-related humorous information to appear when students interact with the candidates.

For example, clicking a candidate can open a short, funny campaign-style explanation of why the campaign believes they are not the right choice.

IMPORTANT:

Do not invent serious accusations, false claims, or defamatory information about the candidates.

The humorous explanations should be obviously playful and based on harmless campaign jokes.

The Fasih option should ultimately lead toward a positive campaign confirmation.

VISUAL STYLE

The entire website should feel EXTREMELY premium.

Think:

Apple keynote

Apple Store

visionOS

modern luxury technology

high-end interactive portfolio

Use:

- White and black as the foundation

- Subtle glassmorphism

- Soft gradients

- Large typography

- Smooth rounded cards

- Elegant shadows

- Subtle depth

- High-quality icons

- Sophisticated transitions

- Excellent spacing

- Minimal interface

- Beautiful micro-interactions

Do NOT make it look like a generic school election website.

No cheesy political graphics.

No clip-art aesthetic.

No excessive colors.

No giant blocks of text.

No clutter.

IPAD EXPERIENCE

The entire website must be designed specifically for an iPad.

Everything must be touch-friendly.

Use:

- Large buttons

- Large candidate cards

- Touch animations

- No hover-only interactions

- No tiny text

- No keyboard input

- No unnecessary scrolling

- Fast transitions

- Portrait and landscape support

The experience should be extremely quick because many students will use the same iPad one after another.

A student should be able to understand what to do immediately.

PERFORMANCE

The website must load extremely quickly.

Avoid unnecessary libraries and heavy assets.

Animations should be smooth but short.

Do not make students wait through long transitions.

The website should feel instantaneous.

IMPORTANT CAMPAIGN DETAIL

This is intentionally a campaign presentation.

The campaign's preferred choices are:

🐊 Muhammad Fasih Ur Rehman

🦚 Azla

The other candidates should still be shown clearly, but the interactive campaign content can humorously explain why the campaign thinks students should consider the preferred candidates.

Keep the humor harmless, clever, and obviously campaign-related.

Do not fabricate factual accusations or personal allegations.

RELOADING

There should be NO reset button.

After a student finishes, I will simply reload the webpage before showing it to the next student.

Therefore, when the page initially loads, everything must return automatically to its original state.

FINAL IMPRESSION

The website should make students think:

"This isn't a school election website. This looks like Apple made an election experience."
